Capability map

Seven modules, one operating system

Each module stands on its own for rollout clarity, yet shares wallets, roles, and reporting so One Touch Solution runs one product — not seven disconnected tools.

PG
Collections
Payment Gateway

Multi-rail acceptance with settlement and merchant controls tuned for your channel — not a generic checkout widget dropped into the portal.

  • UPI, cards, net banking, wallets
  • Webhooks and live transaction status
  • Automated T+1 settlement flow
  • Merchant onboarding and KYC hooks
  • MDR / scheme charge configuration
  • Transaction analytics for ops
POS
Devices
Point of Sale

Full machine lifecycle — stock, assign, rent, transact, and settle — with bridge actions for day-to-day device operations.

  • Inventory and bulk machine upload
  • Assignment to merchants / retailers
  • Bridge ops: pay, refund, void
  • Rental plans with auto-debit logic
  • Card, UPI, BharatQR, cash modes
  • Commission-aware T+1 settlement
QR
Acceptance
QR Code Payments

Instant merchant acceptance via static and amount-locked dynamic QR, with notifications and reconciliation in the same portal.

  • Static QR linked to merchant VPA
  • Dynamic QR for invoiced amounts
  • Real-time payment confirmation
  • Merchant QR history dashboard
  • Settlement and recon reports
  • Branded merchant QR output
MDS
Top tier
Master Distributor

Command view over distributors, wallets, schemes, and downstream performance for the people running your channel at the top.

  • Distributor onboarding and control
  • Wallet funding and balance ops
  • Scheme and commission setup
  • Hierarchy-wide transaction reports
  • Service enablement downstream
  • Performance dashboards
DS
Mid tier
Distributor

The working layer for building and funding a retailer network — with clear earning and monitoring tools.

  • Retailer onboarding and KYC
  • Top-up and fund transfer to retailers
  • Commission and earning reports
  • Retailer-wise monitoring
  • Service control for retailers
  • Settlement and recon views
RTL
Edge
Retailer Portal

Where daily volume happens: enabled services, wallet balance, receipts, and dispute paths without calling the head office for every step.

  • BBPS, AEPS, DMT, recharge access
  • Live digital wallet balance
  • History with receipt generation
  • Mini-statement and balance enquiry
  • Dispute filing and tracking
  • Profile and KYC self-serve
SUB
Governance
Sub-Administrator Panel

Let operations staff work without handing over full super-admin keys — permissions stay department-scoped and auditable.

  • RBAC across wallets, MDR, reports
  • Department-scoped access
  • Scoped user management
  • Activity log and audit trail
  • Reversal / dispute rights by role
  • Custom report access per team
